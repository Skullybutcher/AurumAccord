# Product scope and constraints

## Proposed first workflow

1. Create one gold job card for a jeweller and karigar.
2. Record the agreed terms and issue entry.
3. Record the return measurement and any remaining-material category.
4. Compare the entries using visible, deterministic arithmetic.
5. Open a neutral clarification thread for missing or mismatched values.
6. Prepare one monthly statement and ask both parties to approve the same version.

## Proposed MVP scope

- Shared jeweller–karigar job card.
- Confirmation-first ledger; corrections are new entries and do not silently overwrite earlier records.
- Issue and return entries.
- Discrepancy thread and focused clarification prompts.
- Rules-based plan panel and typed AI drafting, displayed as Suggested until confirmed.
- Monthly statement with dual approval.

## Planned or simulated

- Planned: offline draft queue; voice input; English plus one Indian language.
- Simulated in the presentation: reminders.
- A connection is required for approvals.

The presentation calls the central workflow a “Demo”; this starter does not contain application code and does not claim that the software has been built or tested.

## Product guardrails

- Show quantities, units, inputs, and arithmetic so both parties can review them.
- Do not make a universal wastage determination or infer fault from a difference.
- Do not automatically deduct gold or money, approve a settlement, or execute payment.
- Keep corrections and approvals visible in the record.
- Keep the Job 12 quantities labelled as illustrative, not industry benchmarks.
- Treat local workflow, feature adoption, and willingness to pay as unvalidated assumptions.
