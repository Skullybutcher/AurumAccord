# Supporting research references

These are the external references listed on the presentation’s final slide. They provide sector, labour, policy, or legal context; they do not establish that Aurum Accord’s workflow is used locally, validate market demand, or define a universal wastage allowance.

1. **World Gold Council (2022), “India’s gold jewellery market structure.”** Background on the Indian jewellery market and manufacturing context. [Read the report](https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/indias-gold-jewellery-market-structure)
2. **India Gold Policy Centre, IIMA & XLRI (2023), “HRM Practices, Working Conditions and Labour Engagement in the Indian Gold Jewellery Manufacturing Sector.”** Includes study context and case studies such as Ankurhati Karigar Park and Hunarshala. [Read the research report (PDF)](https://www.iima.ac.in/sites/default/files/2024-04/IGPC%20funded%20project%20BVIIMAJKJXLRI-%20Final%20Report%20June2023%20%281%29.pdf)
3. **NITI Aayog (2018), “Transforming India’s Gold Market.”** Historical policy context; figures should be treated as dated. [Open the official NITI Aayog report page](https://www.niti.gov.in/node/748)
4. **Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, Section 143 (job-work procedure).** Legal context for registered job-work; it is not a description of informal workshop practice or a product rule. [Open the official CBIC tax information page](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-act/1000420/1000001/Job%20work/ACTS)
5. **Vega Jewellery v. ACIT, ITAT Ahmedabad (2017), ITA 2874/Ahd/2015.** A case-specific decision concerning records; it does not set a universal wastage benchmark. [Read the case](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/118177546/)

The idea remains at the submission stage. No field interviews or local adoption validation are claimed in this package.
